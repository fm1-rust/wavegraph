wavegraph-fm1/samples/rave/NOTICE.txt

WaveGraph FM-1 factory rave samples

All six audio assets are available under CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

horn.s16le   "DJ Airhorn" by neopolitansixth (Helm synthesizer)
https://freesound.org/people/neopolitansixth/sounds/547020/

zap.s16le    "LASRGun_Retro Laser Zap Synth_Funky Audio_Sonic Spices" by Funky_Audio
https://freesound.org/people/Funky_Audio/sounds/729389/

stab.s16le   "Synth Stab 1" by Stereo Surgeon
https://freesound.org/people/Stereo%20Surgeon/sounds/265675/

These three excerpts use the high-quality Ogg previews, mixed to mono,
high-pass filtered at 20 Hz with short fades and peak leveling. The horn
and first zap use 22,059 Hz. The G2 stab uses 14,706 Hz.

rise.ima     "Rave Rise" by WaveGraph contributors, dedicated to CC0.
An original oscillator sweep with two echoes, rendered by rise.py.
Inspired by "Pulsar City Alarm" by Adams & Fleisner and its use in
"King of the Beats" by Mantronix. Audio length 1.32 seconds.

amen.ima and amen-compact.ima
             "Amen Break: No Copyright Remake (120BPM)" by JapanYoshiTheGamer
https://freesound.org/people/JapanYoshiTheGamer/sounds/576561/
The author programmed the remake from scratch in FL Studio. Rhythm after
Gregory C. Coleman on The Winstons' "Amen, Brother". The firmware plays
the opening four bars from the Ogg preview. At C4 their speed follows
the transport (122 BPM by default). The default bank uses 98,400 frames
at 12,505 Hz. The original-rate packed bank uses 192,000 at 24,400 Hz.
Both use a single peak gain across all four bars and short boundary
fades. The break and rise use 64-byte mono IMA ADPCM blocks.


house-m7.s16le  "House m7" by WaveGraph contributors, dedicated to CC0.
Original synthesized D4 minor seventh (D, F, A, C) with FM tines,
detuned reeds, a lower D and two short echoes. No record audio was used.
house_m7.py renders the 8,000 mono PCM16 frames at 16 kHz as a WAV.
The .s16le asset is its data chunk.

lofi.py generates house-m7-lofi.ima, stab-lofi.ima and zap-lofi.ima
from the credited PCM masters with FFmpeg. It low-pass filters and
resamples to 8,000, 7,353 and 14,706 Hz respectively, then quantizes
to 10, 8 and 12 bits before encoding as 64-byte mono IMA blocks.
